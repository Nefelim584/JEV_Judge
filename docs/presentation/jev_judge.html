Findings

What the tests revealed

JEV answers from its own knowledge

With the passage removed it still gets 81% on FEVER (majority 68%). A risk for a faithfulness judge.

JEV is not deterministic

The same request twice: probabilities differ by up to 0.11, and 2% of answers flip.

jev-lite: domain shift

Too strict on unseen domains; calibration breaks (ECE 0.03 → 0.30).

jev-lite: refusals

Detecting unanswerable questions does not transfer to new data (0.49).
