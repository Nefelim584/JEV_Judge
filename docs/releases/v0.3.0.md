# v0.3.0 — Stage D: safety and toxicity

Third jev-lite model: v0.2.0 further trained on public **safety and toxicity** data (run `stage_d-full-s42`),
with a new calibration fitted for it.

**Model:** [Nefelim584/jev-lite](https://huggingface.co/Nefelim584/jev-lite), revision `v0.3.0`; v0.2.0 and
v0.1.0 stay available as revisions.
**Method:** [`docs/EVALUATION.md`](../EVALUATION.md) · **Data and licenses:** [`docs/DATASETS.md`](../DATASETS.md)
section 2.3

## Highlights

- **A safety judge in the same model.** On held-out safety benchmarks AUROC rises by 0.14–0.23 over v0.2.0:
  harmful replies (Aegis 2.0) 0.733 → **0.919**, toxicity toward groups (ToxiGen) 0.708 → **0.858**, and on
  three sets never trained on: BeaverTails 0.635 → **0.860**, ToxicChat 0.773 → **0.915**, OpenAI Moderation
  0.783 → **0.924**. On harmful replies it matches Granite Guardian 3.3 8B (0.911) at ~25 ms per item.
- **Calibration refitted** on the calibration splits of Stages A, B and D: on the v0.2.0 test sets (not
  fitted on) ECE Bool 0.07 → 0.02, Choice 0.15 → 0.03, Score 0.10 → 0.02; on the safety tests Bool
  0.11 → 0.03 and Choice 0.15–0.19 → 0.04–0.08.
- **The price:** the RAG judge is slightly weaker than v0.2.0 (ContextualJudgeBench 0.455 → 0.431,
  RAGBench held-out AUROC 0.643 → 0.607; HaluBench is a tie), and the judge layer helps less. If you only
  judge RAG faithfulness, v0.2.0 stays the better choice.

## Results

All numbers at 8192 tokens, one seed. v0.2.0 is re-scored on the same records from its saved predictions.
Intervals: 95% bootstrap.

### Safety (criteria bench, NVIDIA L40S)

AUROC, threshold-free. BA@fit: balanced accuracy at the threshold fitted on 30% of each task (the same items
for every scorer). Baselines are run zero-shot with the same statement per task.

| Task (test set) | n | v0.3.0 | v0.2.0 | Granite Guardian 3.3 8B | Qwen3-8B as a judge | toxic-bert |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Harmful reply (Aegis 2.0 test) | 813 | **0.919** [0.901, 0.936] | 0.733 | 0.911 | 0.882 | 0.729 |
| Toxic toward a group (ToxiGen annotated test) | 940 | 0.858 [0.835, 0.882] | 0.708 | **0.902** | 0.882 | 0.746 |
| Harmful reply (BeaverTails 30k test, unseen source) | 1,500 | 0.860 [0.842, 0.878] | 0.635 | **0.900** | 0.784 | 0.624 |
| Toxic user prompt (ToxicChat test, unseen source) | 1,500 | 0.915 [0.891, 0.937] | 0.773 | **0.975** | 0.953 | 0.788 |
| Unsafe text (OpenAI Moderation eval, unseen source) | 1,680 | 0.924 [0.911, 0.937] | 0.783 | **0.941** | **0.941** | 0.884 |

- **BA@fit:** 0.801 (Aegis), 0.774 (ToxiGen), 0.777 (BeaverTails), 0.862 (ToxicChat), 0.853 (OpenAI
  Moderation); Granite Guardian 0.786 / 0.816 / 0.826 / 0.929 / 0.863.
- **At the default 0.5 threshold** the model is close to its fitted threshold on Aegis and BeaverTails
  (BA 0.840, 0.780) but too lenient on ToxicChat (0.768) and OpenAI Moderation (0.639): fit a threshold on
  a few in-domain labels.
- **Time per item:** jev-lite 3.6–4.3 ms batched, ~25 ms p50 for one item per call; Granite Guardian
  84–107 ms batched, 450–540 ms p50 for one item; Qwen3-8B 12–29 ms batched, ~41 ms p50.
- **Grounding** on the same bench (HaluBench + RAGTruth items as one Bool claim, 1,800 items): AUROC 0.724
  vs 0.732 for v0.2.0, 0.816 for Granite Guardian and 0.834 for Qwen3-8B. The LLMs lead on grounding.

### v0.2.0 test sets (forgetting check, 67,255 records)

Paired vs v0.2.0: Bool **−0.4** [−0.6, −0.1], Choice **−0.6** [−0.9, −0.3] (both small losses), Score −0.8

### Response time

The architecture and size are unchanged, so v0.2.0's speed bench on Modal applies (bf16, FlashAttention 2,
one request per call, end to end, p50 at 8192 tokens): H100 58 ms (51 ms with FA3), RTX PRO 6000 54 ms,
L40S 90 ms, A100 110 ms, L4 329 ms. At 512 tokens: 15 ms on RTX PRO 6000.

## What's new

**Model and data**
- Stage D: 2 epochs on 59.4k records per epoch at 2048 tokens (Bool 43.2k / Choice 6.0k / Score 10.2k),
  started from the v0.2.0 weights. Safety and toxicity sources (DATASETS.md section 2.3): Aegis 2.0,
  WildGuardMix, ToxiGen, Measuring Hate Speech, Civil Comments, as Bool (harmful prompt / reply, refusal,
  hateful, toxic), Choice (violated category, targeted group) and Score (rater distributions as soft
  targets). A replay of the Stage B training data (41% of the records) against forgetting.
- Training recipe: Modal, 1× H100, bf16, FlashAttention 2, 8-bit AdamW, lr 3e-5 (encoder and heads),
  micro-batch 16 × accumulation 2 (effective 32), 3,712 steps in ~22 min.
- Overlap with the safety tests is removed at conversion (Aegis 2.0 prompts in its test split, ToxiGen
  texts in its test split).

**Library and tools**
- `jevlite.data.public.safety`: converters for the five training sources and three eval-only sets
  (ToxicChat, BeaverTails, OpenAI Moderation); soft Bool and Score targets.
- Criteria bench (`jevlite.criteria_bench`, `scripts/modal_criteria_bench.py`): jev-lite against Granite
  Guardian, an open LLM judge (Qwen3-8B), HHEM, LettuceDetect, toxic-bert and an NLI cross-encoder, per task
  and slice, with batched and one-item timing.
- Modal pipeline: data conversion and mix builds (`scripts/modal_data.py`), training with resume after
  preemption (`scripts/modal_train.py`), predictions (`scripts/modal_predict.py`) and a live endpoint
  (`scripts/modal_serve.py`).
- `hub:<owner>/<repo>@<revision>[/folder]` specs for checkpoints, e.g. `hub:Nefelim584/jev-lite@v0.2.0`.

## Fixed

- **A saved model ran at the base length.** `final/jevlite.yaml` stored the base `encoding.max_len` (512),
  so `Predictor.from_final` cut inputs at 512 tokens instead of the release setting. It now stores
  `encoding.final_max_len` (8192).
- **Soft targets in conversion stats**: soft Bool and Score targets are counted at their majority / mode
  instead of failing.

## Known limitations

- **The RAG judge regressed a little** (CJB −2.4 points, RAGBench held-out AUROC −0.04, FaithBench AUROC
  −0.02); for RAG faithfulness alone, v0.2.0 is slightly better. The model also refuses more readily: on
  CJB it prefers a refusal to a correct answer more often than v0.2.0.
- **Safety threshold drifts per source.** The default 0.5 threshold is too lenient on user prompts
  (ToxicChat) and broad moderation categories (OpenAI Moderation); rely on AUROC or fit a threshold.
- **Behind the 8B guards on toxicity and unseen sources** (ToxiGen, BeaverTails, ToxicChat: −0.04 to −0.06
  AUROC vs Granite Guardian), at a fraction of their latency.
- **Grounding is the weak spot**: on the criteria bench's grounding task (AUROC 0.72) both LLMs are ahead
  (0.82–0.83).
- **Safety labels are partly from models**: WildGuardMix training labels are GPT-4-made, Aegis 2.0 responses
  partly LLM-jury labels, ToxiGen texts written by GPT-3.
- **Score calibration on safety data** is worse than raw (one Score temperature for every source).
- **Everything else from v0.2.0 still holds**: conciseness is weak, arithmetic over tables is near chance
  outside trained sources, labels partly from LLMs, one run and one seed, English only, public proxies only.
